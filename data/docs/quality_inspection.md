# Quality Inspection Guidelines

Fictional sample document for demonstration only.

## Incoming material inspection

Inspect incoming material against the purchase order and the specification. Check quantity, labeling and visible damage. Sample parts according to the sampling plan with an acceptable quality limit (AQL) of 1.0 for critical dimensions and 2.5 for cosmetic defects.

## Nonconforming product

Any product that fails inspection must be marked with a red hold tag and moved to the quarantine area. Never mix it with good stock. Raise a nonconformance report (NCR) within 24 hours. The NCR must describe the defect, the quantity affected and the work order number.

## Gauges and measuring equipment

All gauges and measuring tools must be calibrated every 6 months. Check the calibration sticker before using a tool. If the sticker is expired or missing, do not use the tool. Report it to the quality department. Drop or damaged gauges must be removed from service and recalibrated before reuse.

## Torque checks

Torque wrenches used on assembly lines must be verified at the start of every shift with the torque tester. Record the reading on the shift sheet. If a reading is out of tolerance, stop using that wrench and tag it for calibration.
