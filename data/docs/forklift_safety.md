# Forklift Safety Rules

Fictional sample document for demonstration only.

## Who may operate a forklift

Only operators with a valid forklift certificate may drive a forklift. Certificates must be renewed every three years. Never let an uncertified person drive, even for a short distance.

## Pre-shift inspection

Before each shift, inspect the forklift: tires, forks, chains, mast, horn, lights, brakes, steering and the seat belt. Check the fluid or battery level. If you find any defect that affects safety, do not use the forklift. Tag it out and report it to your supervisor.

## Driving rules

The speed limit is 8 km/h in aisles and 5 km/h in pedestrian zones. Always wear your seat belt. Sound the horn at intersections and before reversing. Carry loads with the forks 10 to 15 cm above the ground and the mast tilted slightly back. Never drive with the load raised high. Slow down on wet floors and on ramps.

## Loads

Never exceed the rated capacity shown on the capacity plate. Make sure the load is stable and centered on the forks. Do not lift people on the forks or on pallets.

## Parking

When parking, lower the forks fully to the floor, set the parking brake, turn off the engine or power, and remove the key. Never park in front of fire exits, emergency equipment or electrical panels.
