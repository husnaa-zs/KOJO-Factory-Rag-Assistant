CH.02 仕組み

How it works

This is RAG: retrieval-augmented generation. The AI answers from your documents, not from memory.

ドン
1

Chunk

Documents are cut into small pieces so the right paragraph can be found.

2

Embed

A Hugging Face model turns each piece into numbers that capture its meaning.

3

Retrieve

Your question is embedded too, and the closest pieces are picked by similarity.

4

Answer

Gemini writes the answer using only those pieces and cites them as [1], [2].

Why "I could not find this"?

If even the best piece is a weak match, the app skips the AI and says so. That stops made-up answers about safety procedures.
